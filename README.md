# 🎯 Event Overview

**BUILD YOUR OWN AI AGENT** is a 4-hour guided workshop designed to introduce participants to the fundamentals of AI agents and provide practical experience in building them using No-Code platforms like **Zapier**.

Unlike a traditional theoretical lecture, this event focuses on **learning by doing**.

Participants will understand how AI agents are designed, explore different platforms, and follow along as instructors build two fully functional AI agents live. Finally, participants will have dedicated time to build their own custom agents and learn how to showcase their new skills on LinkedIn to boost their personal brand.

---

# 🧠 Event Objective

The primary objective is to provide participants with a stress-free, practical introduction to AI agent development.

Participants are expected to:

- Understand the concept and architecture of AI agents.
- Explore different AI platforms and integrations.
- Learn how to use **Zapier** to connect AI models with everyday apps (Gmail, Slack, Sheets, etc.).
- Follow along with live demonstrations to build functional agents.
- Build their own custom AI agent during the hands-on session.
- Understand the importance of personal branding and share their workshop experience on LinkedIn.

---

# 🤖 What Is an AI Agent?

An **AI agent** is a system that uses artificial intelligence to understand a task, make decisions, use available tools or resources, and produce an appropriate result.

A basic AI application may simply respond to a user's prompt. An AI agent goes further by:

1. Understanding the user's objective.
2. Following defined instructions.
3. Accessing relevant information.
4. Using connected tools (via platforms like Zapier).
5. Performing actions automatically.
6. Producing a useful final result.

# 🗺️ Workshop Roadmap

Participants will follow a structured learning journey throughout the event.

## 1️⃣ Introduction to AI Agents & Platforms
We will kick off by breaking down the core components of AI agents. We will explore various platforms available in the market and discuss when to use code vs. no-code solutions.

## 2️⃣ The Power of Zapier
We will introduce **Zapier**, the ultimate no-code automation tool. Participants will learn how to set up triggers and actions to connect Large Language Models with thousands of everyday applications.

## 3️⃣ Live Build #1 (Instructor-Led)
Instructors will build the first AI agent live. This will be a foundational agent (e.g., an automated email responder or lead categorizer) to demonstrate how quickly an agent can be deployed.

## 4️⃣ Live Build #2 (Instructor-Led)
A more advanced live build, introducing complex logic, multi-step workflows, or external knowledge retrieval.

## 5️⃣ Hands-on Building
It's your turn! Participants will use the concepts they've learned to build their own AI agent. Instructors will be available to help debug and brainstorm ideas.

## 6️⃣ Personal Branding & LinkedIn
Building skills is only half the battle—you need to show them off! We will discuss the importance of personal branding for tech professionals and guide participants in crafting a compelling LinkedIn post about the AI agent they just built.

# 💡 Suggested Agent Ideas (For Hands-on Session)

Not sure what to build? Here are some beginner-friendly ideas you can create using Zapier:

## 🎓 Study Assistant
An agent that automatically summarizes new emails from professors and adds key deadlines to your Google Calendar.

## 💼 Career Assistant
An agent that monitors job boards via RSS, uses AI to analyze if the job matches your skills, and drafts a cover letter for you.

## 🧑‍💻 Bug Tracker
An agent that takes a simple form input describing a software bug, asks AI to suggest a fix, and logs it into a Trello board.

## ⚡ Personal CRM
An agent that takes new LinkedIn connections, uses AI to draft a personalized welcome message, and saves their details to a Google Sheet.
